Privacy

RAG Triage Demo · last updated 1 October 2026

This is a personal portfolio demonstration, not a commercial product. It is operated by an individual, it has no company behind it, and it may be switched off at any time. Please do not send it anything confidential.

What the demo does with the text you type

Your message is sent to Google Vertex AI (Gemini) to classify it and draft a reply. Before that happens, the message is screened locally and anything that looks like a payment card, email address or phone number is redacted — the model receives the redacted version, not the original.

The message text and the resulting decision are written to a local file on the server so the decision can be reviewed afterwards. That storage is ephemeral: the service scales to zero when idle, and the container's disk is destroyed with it. Nothing is written to a database and nothing is backed up.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It exists only to raise your message allowance; the demo works without it. If you do sign in, Google provides three things:

DataWhere it goes
Your Google account IDA cookie in your browser, and an in-memory counter
Your email addressThe same cookie, so the page can show who is signed in
Your nameThe same cookie

The cookie (rag_sess) is signed so it cannot be altered, but it is not encrypted — anyone holding it could read the email inside. It is marked HttpOnly, so page scripts cannot read it, and it expires after 30 days. The message counter lives in the server's memory and is lost whenever the service restarts.

Your identity is not attached to the messages you send. The decision records described above contain no account information.

What is not done

  • No analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or disclosed to anyone.
  • No profile is built about you, and nothing is used to train a model.

Other parties involved

Google Cloud hosts the service and processes your message through Vertex AI, under Google's own terms. Google Identity handles sign-in; the demo never sees your password. Server logs — request timing and the classification result, not your message text — are retained by Google Cloud Logging under that project's default retention.

Deleting your data

Sign out, or clear your cookies, and the browser-side record is gone. The server-side counter disappears on the next restart, which happens whenever the service has been idle. To have anything else removed, email the address below and it will be done.
